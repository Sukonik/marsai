MARS AI

ONE GALAXY. INFINITE STORIES.

A browser-first personal AI companion presented as an original starship and life-progression RPG. Your life. Your ship. Your universe.

Powers on your crew, voice, and web data. A small on-device AI (about 400 MB, once) loads in the background. Everything can be switched off in Subsystems.

CREW

A crew of distinct celestial personalities, each with a permanent sigil, voice, and role. One captain profile follows you across every conversation.

MISSIONS

Goals become missions. Accomplishments become experience. New habits, projects, and discoveries become part of the captain's continuing story.

SHIPCORE

One underlying system, many voices. ShipCore keeps personality, voice, intelligence provider, and capabilities separate so your journey survives provider changes.

ROADMAP

MARSAI is at the concept and first-prototype stage. The bridge HUD you can power on here is a local demo with no AI provider connected.
